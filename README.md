# OXAI 2026

Primary workshop website: https://oxai2026.github.io/

The old workshop address https://threadslab.org/oxai2026/ forwards to this site. https://naser.github.io/oxai2026/ first redirects to that old address. The THREAD Lab homepage is independent.

Static HTML and CSS, published through GitHub Pages from the root of `main`.

Publish workshop content only to `oxai2026/oxai2026.github.io` (`origin` in this checkout). The separate `naser/oxai2026` repository serves a forwarding page; its local checkout is `../website-redirect`. Do not push the full site to that repository.

## Content

- The local OXAI_2026_FDraft.pdf proposal is the source for the 2026 scope, five topic areas, and organizer details.
- The organizer's September 28 instructions replace the proposal's contribution format: talk abstracts up to 2 pages and papers up to 5 pages. Limits cover the complete PDF, including all text, tables, figures, references, appendices, and other content. The workshop remains non-archival.
- Both submission types use IEEE double-column conference format (24pt title, 10pt body; IEEEtran conference class without compsoc/compsocconf). Brief guidance covers author affiliations and public profiles, disclosure of AI-generated content, and registration/presentation by an author of each accepted contribution. CASCON technical-track page limits and IEEE Xplore publication promises do not apply to this workshop page.
- Originality policy clarified by the organizer on September 28: talk abstracts may describe previously published work with a citation/link; papers must be original and previously unpublished.
- Organizer instructions override the proposal deadlines: submissions October 18, 2026 (AoE); notifications October 23, 2026.
- The October 5, 2024 archived OXAI site informs the call-first structure. CMT site request 27138 was approved. Submissions are open at https://cmt3.research.microsoft.com/OXAI2026; organizer email addresses are for questions only.
- Paper Submission and Edit Submission are enabled in CMT until October 19, 2026, 05:00 Pacific Daylight Time, covering the full October 18 AoE deadline. CMT's time selector uses quarter-hour increments. Notification and final-material stages are separate from opening submissions.
- No Program navigation or schedule is published. The organizer selected November 10, 2026, the first day of CASCON programming, as the workshop date. Final materials are due one week earlier, November 3.
- CASCON's official 2026 site supplies conference and registration links. Its overall dates are November 9–12; CSER is November 9, followed by CASCON November 10–12.

Edit `index.html` for content and `styles.css` for presentation. There is no build step or dependency installation.
